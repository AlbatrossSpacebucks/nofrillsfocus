No Frills Focus

A digital minimalism environment for macOS.

What it is

No Frills Focus masks everything on your screen except the work you chose.

Nothing gets moved or replaced, just hidden from view.

How it works

When you start a session, your work appears in a standard macOS window — the same size Apple uses for reading, writing, and browsing. Everything else fades behind a neutral backdrop. What remains is a single, quiet workspace.

Use the writing tools and apps you already trust.

How sessions work

Open your work document.
Choose a 15, 30, or 60 minute session.
Or choose “until I’m done.”
Press "Get started."

There’s no visible timer, no countdown anxiety — just a quiet end when the session is over.

Getting out

You can exit a live session at any time — we just make not working a conscious decision rather than a passive one.

  • Normal exit: Cmd + Shift + X
  • Emergency quit: Cmd + Shift + Z
Design philosophy

The most effective way to focus attention is with subtraction, not addition.
Constraint and simplicity is the point.
The rest is up to you.

Privacy

We don’t store your data.

Your work always stays on your computer.
