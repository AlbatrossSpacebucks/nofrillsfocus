No Frills Focus

A digital minimalism environment for macOS.

What it is

No Frills Focus masks everything on your screen except the work you're doing. Nothing on your desktop is moved, changed, or rearranged — it’s simply hidden from view.

How it works

Start a session. Choose the document or app you want to work in. Your work appears inside a macOS window. Everything else fades behind a neutral backdrop.

    • No virtual desktops.
    • No workflows to learn.
    • No file juggling.
    • Use the writing tools and work apps you already trust.
    • When the session ends, your desktop returns exactly as you left it.

    Why this helps

    No Frills Focus was built around a simple observation: when visual drift points disappear, your mind stops scanning for exits, your grip on time loosens, and longer work sessions start to feel second nature.

    There’s no visible timer on screen, no countdown anxiety, no score-keeping — just a quiet end when the session is over.

    Design philosophy

    No Frills Focus isn’t about productivity tricks or motivational jargon. It’s about making a deliberate commitment: this is the work I’m doing now. Everything else can wait. The app applies light enforcement to that decision by removing everything else from view. Constraint and simplicity are the point. The rest is up to you.

    Privacy

    No Frills Focus doesn’t track you or collect your data. There are no subscriptions or tiers. Your work stays on your computer. The app works entirely offline.

    Getting out

    You can always end a session early. Nothing is locked. We just make not working a conscious decision rather than a passive one.

    • Cmd + Shift + X — Exit session
    • Cmd + Shift + Z — Exit app
