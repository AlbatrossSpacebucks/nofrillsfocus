# No Frills Focus

## ◼︎ Purpose

No Frills Focus is a digital minimalism tool designed to eliminate visual drift. This is not a window manager, task system, or productivity suite. It is a commitment device to make work sessions frictionless and distraction-free.

---

## ▶︎ Sessions

1. Choose the document or online app you want to work on.
2. Choose a session length.
3. The screen locks into a fixed layout.
4. The layout remains unchanged until the session ends or you exit.

Your document must be open on your desktop before you begin a session in order for the app to see it.

---

## ⊘ macOS Boundaries

No Frills Focus respects macOS system boundaries. Because of this, you may observe the following behaviors, depending on your hardware and OS version:

- **Menu bar safe area:** On some MacBooks (particularly those with notched displays), macOS reserves the menu bar strip at the top of the screen. This area cannot be overridden by third‑party apps.


## ⏎ Exit

Your session is never locked. We simply make not working a deliberate action instead of a passive one. If you need to end a session early, use the exit shortcuts displayed under your document.

---

## ◼︎ Privacy

No Frills Focus does not collect, store, or transmit your data. There are no accounts, subscriptions, or background services. Nothing on your desktop is moved, rearranged, or uploaded. The app works entirely offline.

---

## { } Constraint

The idea behind No Frills Focus is simple: by removing small choices during a session, you gain more freedom to stay with the work itself. There is no resizing, reframing, or rearranging during your session. That impulse is exactly what the app is designed to remove. Constraint is the product.


---

## ℹ︎ Version

Your current version number is displayed in the selection window. This can be useful if macOS updates affect behavior or if you need to confirm you are running the latest release.
